I currently have two rather old albums available on Spotify, Apple Music and Tidal. Released by a German indie label associated with evengelical christian groups. Today, I maintain no links with the label nor identify with this or any other religion and I am not receiving any royalties from the releases either.

The first album, **Bluetronic - Electronic Worship** is a selection of Trance and Techno tracks, some produced for the _Planet Lif_e Techno services I used to co-organise back in Munich with a local protestant church.
