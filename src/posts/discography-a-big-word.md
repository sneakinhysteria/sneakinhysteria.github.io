## Bluetronic & Mylk

For my early work, see [Music 1995 - 2001](/music-1995-2001/)

In 2001 I wrote and produced music for _Stadtheather Heidelberg_ and its dance theatre production **Wahnsinnsjung**, directed by [Irina Pauls](https://www.irinapauls.de/). There is no streamig source for the music, I may make it available some day.

## The Truth About Lies

In Ireland in 2009, A few colleagues and I formed a indie/alt rock cover band called The Truth About Lies. We toured local pubs and had a great time. [Here is a demo medley on Soundcloud.](https://soundcloud.com/the-truth-about-lies/demo-medley?utm_source=clipboard&utm_medium=text&utm_campaign=social_sharing) I played bass, after the rest of the band decided that my voice wasn't quite what they had in mind. They used different words. Still proud that the only song I ever sang as a lead singer was Placebo's Nancy Boy!
